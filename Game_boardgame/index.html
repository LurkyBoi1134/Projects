How to play

You always play as South. Select a highlighted piece, then choose a green destination; North, East, and West are computer-controlled.

Move onto a reachable East or West enemy piece to attack. For ordinary pieces, the lower rank is removed; special pieces follow their listed battle rules.

A railway move can finish with one adjacent road step to reach or attack a stop beside the track.

Player front faces the center: North→South, South→North, East→West, West→East.

Center: each side is a straight line of 3 stops — 2 end stops and 1 middle camp — plus the center stop.

Computer turns play automatically after a short pause.

Turn order runs clockwise: North → East → South → West.
