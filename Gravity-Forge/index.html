How to Play

⌨ Keyboard

  • ← → / A D — walk along the floor
  • ↑ / W / Space — jump
  • Z / Q / [ — rotate gravity CCW
  • X / E / ] — rotate gravity CW
  • R — restart level  ·  M — mute
  • P / Esc — pause  ·  F — fullscreen

📱 Touch

  • Drag left half — virtual joystick (walk)
  • ▲ — jump
  • ● — tap: rotate 90° · drag: set gravity direction

⚡ The core idea

Gravity is a force you aim. You always fall toward it, jump away from it, and walk along whatever surface you land on. Rotate gravity and the whole world spins around your robot.

☠ Hazards

Spikes, laser beams, crushers and falling blocks destroy your robot — but checkpoints keep your progress, and every death is just a lesson. Keys open doors, switches open everything else.

⭐ Scoring

Finish fast for up to 3 stars per level. Best times, stars and deaths are saved automatically.
