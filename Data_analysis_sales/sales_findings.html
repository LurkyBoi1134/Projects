Executive conclusion

Sales climbed; profitability stayed broadly consistent.

Across 9,994 recorded orders, the dataset reports 14,956,982 in sales and 3,747,121.20 in profit, for a 25.1% overall margin. Annual sales increased 67.3% from 2015 to 2018. Eggs, Meat & Fish led sales, while Snacks generated the most profit. Discount bands show similar average margins, but this comparison alone cannot establish a discount effect.

02 / Data preparation

How the data was cleaned

The raw CSV is retained. Cleaning prepares dates and analysis fields while preserving the original order-level records.

1

Load and inspect

Read the 11 source columns, checked data types, null counts, duplicate rows, unique order IDs, and numeric ranges. Found 0 missing cells, 0 duplicate rows, and 9,994 unique IDs.

2

Standardize dates

The source mixes hyphen and slash formats. Hyphenated dates were parsed as day-month-year; slash dates were parsed as month/day/year. All 0 dates parsed successfully (2015-01-02 to 2018-12-30).

3

Create analysis fields

Derived year, month, and profit margin (profit ÷ sales) for grouping and comparison. Kept sales, profit, discounts, and categories unchanged; no rows were dropped or imputed.

Analysis takeaways

Signals in the records

These are descriptive patterns in the available records, not forecasts or causal estimates.

01 — MOMENTUM

Sales grew 67.3%

Annual sales rose from 2.98M in 2015 to 4.98M in 2018. The data shows a strong upward trend across the period.

02 — CATEGORY MIX

Sales leader ≠ profit leader

Eggs, Meat & Fish led sales at 2.27M; Snacks led profit at 568.2K. Revenue rank alone misses differences in profitability.

03 — DISCOUNTS

Margins look steady

Average profit margin ranges from 24.8% to 25.6% across discount bands. This simple comparison does not establish a discount effect.

Read the small print
