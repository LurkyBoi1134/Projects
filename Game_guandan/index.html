At the table

A quick guide to this practice game

  • Partners & dealFour players sit opposite their partner. Two decks are shuffled and dealt 27 cards to each player.
  • Make a playLead any valid combination: single, pair, triple, three pairs, straight, full house, or consecutive triples. Other players must beat the same kind of play or pass.
  • BombsFour or more of a kind beat ordinary plays. A five-card straight flush beats bombs of up to five cards; the four-joker bomb beats everything.
  • Level cardHeart cards of the current level are wild and can stand in for another suited card. Level cards rank above A; jokers rank highest.
  • Win the handThe first partnership to place both players takes the hand. Its level advances by 3 for a 1st–2nd finish, 2 if its partner finishes third, or 1 otherwise. A-level is the finish.

This is a single-player practice table against three basic opponents. Tribute and tournament variations are omitted. Rules reference: Wikipedia: Guandan.
